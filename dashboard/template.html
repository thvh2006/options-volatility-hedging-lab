Hedge frequency and transaction costs

Saved paired experiments. Each cell uses 256 paths with the same underlying price paths across costs and rebalance policies. Quarter-year horizon with 256 calendar-time grid steps. Costs apply to spot trade notional including initial and final trades.

Hedge volatility stays 25%. Error is terminal cash after settling the short option and closing the hedge. No capital-normalised return, Sharpe ratio or universally optimal frequency is inferred.

Separate synthetic evaluation block

256 new seeds per scenario; fixed 25% volatility, two midpoint volatility regimes and a conditional 20% down-jump stress. Compare rebalancing every 4 steps with every step on exactly the same paths. The table shows policy RMSE minus reference RMSE; negative values favour every 4 steps under these assumptions.

Pointwise 95% paired percentile bootstrap intervals, 2,000 resamples. No correction for multiple comparisons and no policy selected. Jump stress imposes one midpoint jump on every path; it estimates no jump arrival rate. New synthetic seeds are not market out-of-sample evidence.
